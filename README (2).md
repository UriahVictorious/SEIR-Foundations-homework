# Lesson 3: Jira, A Day in My Shoes

One day of my life as a Walmart truck driver, broken down in Jira as one epic, 8 stories, and 30 tasks, with a story point estimate on every story and task.

## Estimation scale

Points measure effort compared with my other items, not minutes.

| Points | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 1 | Quick, low effort (a few minutes) |
| 2 | Light effort: short routines, a meal, fueling |
| 3 | Moderate effort: inspections, packing, a longer task |
| 5 | Heavy effort: about an hour of driving, focused study |
| 8 | Most demanding: hours of sustained driving |
